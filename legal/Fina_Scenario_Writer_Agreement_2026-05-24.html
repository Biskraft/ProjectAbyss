Fina Ocampo Sta Ana — based in the Republic of the Philippines. Contact: finaocampo@gmail.com

Fina writes scenario, characters, dialogue, and lore for the video game ECHORIS, in English. In return, Yongtai pays Fina 5% of the net revenue that ECHORIS actually receives — that is, after platform commissions (Steam and any other store), taxes, currency conversion, and transfer costs are deducted. Refund and chargeback costs are absorbed by Yongtai and do not reduce Fina's share. Payment is made quarterly, by international bank transfer, within thirty days after each calendar quarter closes.

The total cumulative payment to Fina under this Agreement is capped at USD $50,000. Once the cap is reached, the two of us may renegotiate for further work or new projects, but neither side is obligated to continue.

All rights to the content Fina writes — including the right to use, modify, translate, and adapt it, and any sequels, expansions, downloadable content, ports, or other derivative works of ECHORIS — belong exclusively to Yongtai.

Fina will be credited in the game's end credits, on the Steam store page, and on the official website. The exact form of credit — for example "Special Thanks", "Written by Fina", or similar — will be decided by Yongtai before release. Fina may use her real name or a handle of her choosing.

Fina may decline to write any content she finds objectionable, without giving a reason. Yongtai will produce such content separately, and Fina's compensation is unchanged.

Fina represents that her writing is her own original work.
