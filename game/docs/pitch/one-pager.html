Every weapon you find hides a procedural world inside it.

Descend a vertical megastructure left by giants who no longer remember why. Step inside any blade you find to enter its Item World. Defeat the memories that linger inside it, slot the shards you reclaim into its Identity, and reforge a weapon no other player will ever wield.
