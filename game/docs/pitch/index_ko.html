ECHORIS
The Spike

Every weapon
hides a world.

Each blade carries a voice — the Ego of the soul who once wielded it. Step inside any weapon to enter its Item World. Descend its Memory Strata. Defeat the bosses that guard each layer. Reforge the weapon into something stronger — again, and again, and again.

Exploration · Forging · Cooperation.

PILLAR 01

Explore

The Wanderer

Unlock relics one by one and reach places you could not reach before. Stat gates and ability gates lock paths until you grow into them. A vertical megastructure unfolds the deeper you go.

ECHORIS
Five Temperaments

No two weapons grow the same way.

Each Memory Shard belongs to one of five temperaments. The mix you choose shapes what kind of soul your blade becomes.

Forge

Rage · Fire

Aggressive, destructive, burns through resistance.

Iron

Resolve · Defense

Unyielding, defensive, holds the line.

Rust

Resignation · Decay

Patient corruption, lingering damage over time.

Spark

Curiosity · Light

Quick, inquisitive, finds what is hidden.

Shadow

Stealth · Silence

Concealed strikes from places that should not exist.

Visual Style

Architecture of
the forgotten.
