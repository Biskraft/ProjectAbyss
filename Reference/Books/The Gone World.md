## **About the book**

In a gripping blend of science fiction and detective noir, *The
Gone World* follows special agent Shannon Moss, who is
tasked with unraveling a brutal murder case that could alter the
fate of humanity. Set in western Pennsylvania in 1997, Moss, a
member of a secretive division within the Naval Criminal
Investigative Service, investigates the shocking murder of a
Navy SEAL's family and the disappearance of his teenage
daughter. As she grapples with the constraints of conventional
law enforcement, Moss uncovers dark secrets surrounding the
missing SEAL’s journey aboard the U.S.S. Libra, a spaceship
presumed lost in the depths of time. Utilizing her own
harrowing experiences with time travel, she traverses potential
futures in search of answers, ultimately facing a chilling
revelation: her investigation may be tied to an impending
cataclysm known as the Terminus, a calamity poised to wipe
out humanity. Blending profound themes with human
emotion, *The Gone World* is a thought-provoking, tense
narrative that explores the intersection of time, fate, and the
fragility of existence.


## **About the author**

Tom Sweterlitsch is an American author known for his unique
blend of science fiction, mystery, and philosophical
exploration, often reflecting on the nature of existence and the
complexities of time. With a background in film and
television, Sweterlitsch brings a cinematic quality to his
writing, evident in his debut novel, "The Gone World," which
intricately weaves a tale of time travel and alternate realities.
His work is characterized by a deep sense of atmosphere and
psychological depth, drawing readers into thought-provoking
narratives that challenge the boundaries of genre. In addition
to his novels, Sweterlitsch has also contributed to various
anthologies and magazines, solidifying his reputation as a
versatile storyteller in contemporary speculative fiction.

## **Chapter 1 Summary : Prologue**

**Summary of Chapter 1: The Gone World**


**Setting the Stage: A Dark Future**


In the chilling prologue set in the year 2199, we meet
Shannon, a trainee on her first expedition to a grim future
Earth. The landscape is a haunting winter forest, featuring
blackened trees and two suns—the familiar sun and a
mysterious entity known as the White Hole. As Shannon
walks through the frozen terrain, her suit protects her from
the cold, but she’s warned that the environment is hazardous,
filled with quantum-tunneling nanoparticles (QTNs) that
could infect her.


**The Descent into Madness**


As Shannon navigates the eerie woods, she becomes
disoriented and frightened. Strange phenomena occur,
including a vision of another version of herself, a woman
crucified upside down above a black river. Disturbed by this
horrifying scene, Shannon grapples with her sanity, fearing
that the QTNs are warping her reality. In her panic, she loses
her way, and as she witnesses multiple crucifixions along the
river, a sense of despair envelops her.


**Desperate Rescue**


Finally, a Quad-lander, named Theseus, arrives to rescue her.
Despite her longing to return to the scene where she saw
herself being tortured, Shannon is extricated from the
desolate future and wakes up in a medical pod aboard the
lander. Here, she faces her physical injuries—frostbite and
burns from her haunting experience. Her instructor, a caring
yet evasive presence, attempts to soothe her, but Shannon is
overwhelmed with confusion and pain.


**The Reality of QTNs and the Terminus**


As she struggles to comprehend her ordeal, the instructor
explains the QTNs and their connection to the "Terminus," a
catastrophic event that marks the end of humanity's
relevance. Shannon learns that countless timelines have
witnessed the Terminus, which is continuously approaching
mankind. Despite her yearning for clarity, her memories
remain muddled, and she feels an unsettling connection to
the gruesome visions she experienced.


**Conclusion: A Fight Against Fate**


Shannon's fears deepen as she contemplates the implications
of QTNs and the grim fate awaiting humanity. A haunting
line echoes in her mind as she grapples with the reality of her
experience: "The end of everything." This introduction not
only sets the tone for the eerie journey ahead but also raises
important questions about fate, survival, and the nature of
reality in a world overshadowed by an impending doom.

## **Chapter 2 Summary : PART ONE**

**Summary of Chapter 2: The Gone World**


In this gripping chapter, we follow Special Agent Shannon
Moss as she is thrust into a horrific crime scene in
Canonsburg, Pennsylvania. The story opens with a tense
phone call from Special Agent Philip Nestor of the FBI,
informing her about a triple homicide involving a Navy
SEAL family and the mysterious disappearance of their
seventeen-year-old daughter, Marian Mursult. Moss is drawn
back to her childhood memories, especially of her late best
friend, Courtney Gimm, who had lived in the very house now
turned into a crime scene.
Moss’s background as a Naval Criminal Investigative
Service (NCIS) agent is revealed, alongside her physical


struggles following a devastating amputation from her time
in a program called the Terminus. As she arrives at the crime
scene, memories flood her mind—fear, nostalgia, and grief
for her friend Courtney, who was killed years prior. The
scene is brutal; Moss pushes through her emotions to
navigate the horrific reality before her, witnessing the
aftermath of violence that’s both grotesque and familiar.
As Moss investigates, she uncovers troubling details about
the Mursult family, including strange mutilations of the
bodies and the connection to Deep Waters, a clandestine
naval program. The plot thickens with links between Patrick
Mursult, the father, and a mysterious friend named Elric
Fleece, who has similarly dark ties to the naval past. Moss’s
commitment to finding Marian becomes deeply personal,
driven by echoes of her own lost childhood and unresolved
trauma.
Moss learns that Patrick Gannon, an alias for Patrick
Mursult, has frequently stayed in a lodge at Blackwater Falls,
raising alarms about Marian's potential whereabouts. After a
fateful night at the lodge leads to the discovery of Patrick
Mursult's dead body—not from the original crime but
seemingly executed, the urgency escalates to locate the
missing girl.
The chapter explores heavy themes of loss, the lingering


impact of trauma, and the weight of responsibility as Moss
grapples with her past while trying to save Marian. As agents
race against time to piece together the mystery, Moss’s
connection to the victims and her haunted past resurface,
driving her forward in a quest not just for justice, but for
redemption.
This chapter sets the stage for an intense exploration of time,
memory, and the intertwining fates of its characters, leaving
readers eager to learn what will become of Marian and how
the past continues to shape the present.
