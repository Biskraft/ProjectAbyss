# Item World Entry Streaming

## Current State

- Shaft/anvil Item World entry still crosses from `LdtkWorldScene` into `ItemWorldScene`; this is prestreaming, not true same-scene map streaming.
- `LdtkWorldScene.init()` seeds `ItemWorldTemplatePool` from the already-parsed `ItemStratum` LDtk world so `ItemWorldScene` does not fetch `World_ProjectAbyss.ldtk` again on entry.
- `LdtkWorldScene` starts entry prestreaming during anvil dive, archived ItemTunnel load/exit, and portal entry. It warms the `item_world` bundle, ItemStratum templates, theme area tilesets, and extra LDtk tilesets.
- `ItemWorldScene` clones cached LDtk templates before theme retagging because `applyAreaTilesetToLdtkTiles()` mutates tile `tilesetPath`.
- ItemStratum tile retagging preserves LDtk-authored non-default tilesets such as `atlas/itemstratum_01.png`; only tiles still authored on the default `atlas/world_01.png` path are retagged to the item theme area tileset.
- Anvil/tunnel handoff skips the redundant second fade-to-black because those paths already reach black before `ItemWorldScene` is pushed; only the reveal fade remains.
- The anvil deployment ghost room is a walkable streamed extension: `LdtkWorldScene` temporarily extends collision rows/camera bounds, stamps ItemStratum air and solids into the extension, and moves the entry trigger inside the ghost room instead of the Shaft edge.
- Procedural `ItemWorldScene` entries can opt into an `ItemStratum_Corridor` interstitial. Overworld entries pass `{ entryCorridor: true }`, load the real LDtk `ItemStratum_Corridor` level from the shared ItemStratum template pool, start the player at the corridor's left air-over-solid tile, then hand off from the corridor right/bottom exit to the start room ceiling at the remembered corridor x-coordinate.
- `ItemStratum_Corridor` renders black platform tiles over the normal Item World parallax background and reveals solid tiles near the player with per-tile scale 0 -> 1 animation. During the corridor, both the corridor container and the parallax background are temporarily filtered to grayscale with contrast 0.5. Do not replace it with a generated grid unless the LDtk level is missing.
- When the entry corridor hands off into the actual Item World start room, preserve the grayscale/contrast look on the world/background layers for 1000 ms, then fade the filter alpha back to normal color over the next 1000 ms.
- During the default anvil growth deployment, `LdtkWorldScene.checkLevelEdges()` is suppressed while `itemDeployment.isActive`; the handoff is owned by the streamed Level 36 right-edge entrance AABB. Non-deployment world-right-edge entries can still start `__item_world__`.
- The old laser-cannon deployment is preserved as `LegacyLaserItemDeploymentController` plus `LegacyAnvilGateLaser`; it is not the default anvil deployment path.
- The default `ItemDeploymentController` is now a hybrid growth sequence: after the player places and strikes the item, `LdtkWorldScene` captures the current background/world/anvil item into a static world snapshot below the live player, hides the original world visuals, then scales that snapshot from 1x to 64x around the placed item's center.
- The default growth sequence first scales only the captured background/world/anvil item snapshot from 1x to 64x. `ItemStratum_Level_36` is not loaded into the runtime collision grid until that zoom finishes, but its LDtk `Player` entity is read at zoom start so the live player can move toward the future Level 36 spawn during the zoom.
- `LdtkWorldScene` also prewarms the hidden `ItemWorldGhostOverlay` for Level 36 at zoom start. This moves the expensive per-tile `Graphics` construction out of the zoom-complete frame; zoom completion should only activate the prebuilt overlay, stamp collision, and unlock movement.
- `ItemWorldGhostOverlay` derives its wall/platform/highlight/rim colors from the placed item PNG. The result is still darkened for gameplay readability, but the generated IntGrid should feel made from the weapon's material instead of using a fixed blue-black palette.
- When the zoom completes, `LdtkWorldScene` creates the streamed `ItemWorldGhostOverlay` from `ItemStratum_Level_36`, stamps that level's full IntGrid into the extended runtime `collisionGrid`, and finalizes the live player at the LDtk `Player` entity in Level 36. The IntGrid tiles start visually hidden at scale 0, so the collision is already real while the terrain is revealed by proximity.
- On Level 36 activation, reveal the initial 6-tile neighborhood around the LDtk `Player` spawn immediately. Otherwise collision exists before the tile scale animation has made the floor visible, making the player appear to float on a straight invisible platform.
- During the default growth deployment, the old overworld `collisionGrid` is temporarily cloned and cleared to air before Level 36 is stamped. This isolates collision testing to authored Level 36 data and prevents Shaft/world terrain from affecting the streamed entry.
- After Level 36 is loaded, the controller immediately unlocks movement in `Deployed`. `ItemWorldGhostOverlay` reveals tiles within 6 tiles of the moving player by scaling each tile from 0 to 1, while the player walks to the right-edge trigger and then enters `ItemWorldScene`.
- The growth reference starts with a 500 ms `Anticipation` beat that shakes the camera before the actual zoom/world-birth begins. Snapshot and ghost scale use a smootherstep growth curve: very slow at the beginning, fastest in the middle, and very slow near completion.
- The default growth path locks player input during zoom. `PlatformReady` loads Level 36 collision and immediately advances to `Deployed`; only the final `EnteringWorld` fade reports `isBlocking`.
- Level 36 tile fills are solid; do not draw per-tile top/bottom strokes because scaled tiles read as visible horizontal scan lines.
- Runtime grid extension for the streamed ghost room must initialize new cells as air before stamping Level 36's IntGrid. The pre-existing overworld collision is also cleared to air during the deployment and restored on cleanup/return.
- `LdtkWorldScene.triggerFloorCollapse()` hides anvil prompts and the world UI immediately before starting the growth deployment. Do this at the trigger point, not only on the next `updateAnvil()` tick, or the `Acquire Item` prompt can remain visible over the cinematic snapshot.
- `LdtkWorldScene.triggerFloorCollapse()` also clears save point prompt/aura UI before capturing the growth snapshot, and `checkSavePoints()` stays suppressed while item deployment is active. Otherwise `SavepointPulseManager` can attach a blue circular aura during the cinematic or get baked into the scaling world snapshot.
- The new growth deployment skips `ItemStratum_Corridor` on final handoff by entering `ItemWorldScene` with `entryCorridor: false`; legacy and other entry paths can still opt into the corridor.
- `LegacyAnvilGateLaser` enters a 1000ms max-width hold after the burst reaches full size, then decays. When the legacy tunnel laser fires, the legacy controller locks camera zoom at 1.0 until Item World handoff or controller destroy; debug zoom keys bypass the lock by updating the locked value.
- The anvil deployment now uses `ItemWorldForgeBirth`: its large item/INTGRID preview starts hidden so no duplicate big item appears at the forge. During `ItemPunch`, the placed anvil item stays in its sprite colors, scales up to the forge preview size, and travels from the anvil placement point to the laser-birth focus; then `ItemWorldForgeBirth` reveals that full-size item for the laser hit.
- `Anvil.placeItem()` shows only the actual item icon on the anvil. It intentionally does not draw the old rarity-colored fallback `Graphics`, which appeared as a white pixel block for normal items.
- The placed anvil item icon, local symbol prompt, and world-space `KeyPrompt` all anchor to `Anvil.getFloorPlateCenterWorld()`, the visual center of the anvil floor plate. Do not center these on raw `anvil.x`, which is the entity pivot and can sit off the plate center.
- On legacy laser burst, `ItemWorldForgeBirth` hitstops and shatters the item sprite into deterministic random-size texture chunks. Screen-glass crack and forge lightning-branch calls are intentionally disabled.
- `ItemWorldForgeBirth` no longer draws a rectangular cyan frame around the item/internal grid. Legacy laser strike shards remain floating after the laser ends with slow, low-amplitude motion; they are released only after the player starts moving into the streamed Item World area. Moving shards duplicate to roughly 2x count, darken toward black during travel, then scale to 0 near the end.
- Legacy laser strike shards pull their floating rest positions 62% toward their scattered target from the item center, keeping the broken weapon mass visually clustered instead of filling the whole camera view.
- In the preserved legacy laser path, `ItemWorldForgeBirth` is anchored in front of the anvil laser source instead of the placed-item icon, so future changes to the visible item placement do not move that legacy focal point.
- On laser burst, deployment pans the camera target 20 tiles to the right to show the tunnel/world formation, then returns to the player 500 ms after `AnvilGateLaser` completes.
- `ItemWorldGhostOverlay` reveal tiles settle into place one at a time at a slower 400 ms scale-in pace. `LdtkWorldScene` copies the full `ItemStratum_Level_36` IntGrid into the streamed runtime `collisionGrid` as soon as Level 36 is loaded, so platforms are physically stable before their visual reveal completes.
- Ghost terrain containers are inserted below the player/entity layer. The reveal is visual-only; collision is fixed up front from the LDtk Level 36 grid.
- `ItemWorldScene` still uses area palette `Weather=stratum`, but `WeatherSystem` now interprets that as weapon-colored rain/snow variants. Do not bring back the old floating mote/spark/fog field for Item World weather.
- ItemWorld wall rim lighting uses `RimLightFilter({ direction: 'bottom' })` so aggregate walls read as lit from below. Keep normal world/shaft rim lighting on the default top-edge direction unless that scene's lighting changes too.
- First-boss return inventory hint can be deferred while the placed item is still on the anvil. If the anvil retires instead of entering the normal reclaim path, clear `lastUsedAnvilItem` and flush `pendingFirstIwReturnHintHadFirstBossClear` after retire so the persistent inventory tutorial still appears.
- The first-boss return inventory hint is complete once `sword_rustborn` is equipped. Dismiss visible inventory tutorial hints, clear pending inventory hint state, and stop the HUD item-key pulse when that equip is detected.

## Prevention Rules

- Do not pre-init a full `ItemWorldScene` while the overworld is still visible; `init()` attaches scene/UI/background state and can leak visuals before `SceneManager.push()`.
- Keep Item World birth/preview FX data-only or visual-only before handoff. Use lightweight template/collision grids for previews, not live `ItemWorldScene` instances.
- Keep the preserved laser path under the `Legacy*` names unless intentionally restoring it; the default anvil entry is the growth reference sequence.
- In the default growth path, keep the static world snapshot pivot on the placed item center and keep the ghost room placement item-pivot based with the 12-tile left visual offset. Do not move them back to the player-floor or tunnel-center pivot unless the visual direction changes again. In the preserved legacy laser path, keep the focal point tied to the forge/laser source.
- Keep the default IntGrid/ghost overlay enabled during anvil growth. The player should end the growth on a safe generated platform and enter Item World by walking to the streamed room's exit trigger, not by an automatic post-growth handoff.
- Keep ghost IntGrid color item-derived: sample the placed item art, darken it into readable dungeon colors, and update the rim/highlight from that palette. Avoid returning to a fixed blue-black ghost palette unless item art cannot be sampled.
- Keep snapshot scale on the growth curve. Do not add a pre-growth hold unless it is intentional and visible as a separate anticipation beat.
- Keep default control unlock after Level 36 is stamped and the player has reached the LDtk `Player` entity. The player should be moved toward that authored target during zoom, not teleported only after zoom completion.
- Keep Level 36 overlay construction prewarmed during zoom. Do not build thousands of ghost tile `Graphics` for the first time in the `PlatformReady` frame.
- Reveal the Level 36 spawn neighborhood immediately when activating collision; proximity reveal can still animate farther tiles as the player moves.
- Keep overworld collision disabled during the default growth deployment until cleanup or Item World handoff restores the saved grid.
- Do not fill streamed ghost extension cells with wall values; extend with air, then stamp Level 36's IntGrid.
- Do not add a separate fake entry path or hidden backstop wall for the default growth entry. Level 36's authored IntGrid and `Player` entity own the playable entry layout.
- Do not add per-tile highlight/shadow strokes to the growth IntGrid tiles; use aggregate filters/rim light instead, or the scaled room reads as striped bands.
- Do not make the default growth states report `isBlocking`; `isBlocking` is reserved for the final handoff fade so growth visuals continue updating while input is locked. Legacy laser deployment may still use blocking states.
- Hide anvil prompts synchronously when starting the default growth cutscene; prompt cleanup that waits for the next proximity/update pass can leak over the transition.
- Hide save point prompt/aura UI synchronously when starting the default growth cutscene and keep save point proximity disabled while `itemDeployment.isActive`; save point aura lives on `entityLayer`, not the normal HUD path.
- Do not reintroduce a generic rarity-colored placed-item fallback on the anvil; missing item art should be fixed in `game/public/assets/items/` instead of drawing a small white block over the anvil.
- Keep anvil placed-item and prompt placement on `Anvil.getFloorPlateCenterWorld()` instead of `anvil.x`; the LDtk/entity pivot is not the visible floor plate center.
- Do not draw fake completed platform chunks in `ItemWorldForgeBirth`. Player-proximity completion belongs to `ItemWorldGhostOverlay`; the streamed `collisionGrid` is copied up front from `ItemStratum_Level_36`.
- Do not delay Level 36 collision behind visual reveal. The grid must be fixed before movement unlocks so the player cannot outrun the construction animation and fall through unrevealed platforms.
- Do not re-enable full-screen stratum breathing haze in Item World; keep color movement local to motes/sparks or other small effects.
- Do not reuse the shaft's top-edge rim direction for ItemWorld wall aggregates; ItemWorld is bottom-lit.
- Keep shared ItemStratum templates immutable. Clone before applying theme-specific tile retags.
- Do not blindly retag every ItemStratum LDtk tile to the area palette tileset. Preserve non-default authored `tilesetPath` values so LDtk-specific Item World tile art appears in game.
- Keep streamed ghost collision scoped and restorable. Restore old row lengths and camera bounds when the ghost overlay is destroyed.
- When releasing overworld visuals for `ItemWorldScene`, restore ghost/tunnel collision without rerendering the tilemap; the return path rebuilds the level with `loadLevel()`.
- Keep the corridor as an `ItemWorldScene` entry state, not a second pre-pushed scene. It pauses normal start-room enemy spawn/dialogue until the corridor completes.
- Restore `game.backgroundContainer.filters` when the corridor completes or the scene exits; otherwise the grayscale/contrast filter leaks into normal Item World rendering.
- Exclude `RoomType=Cinematic` ItemStratum levels from procedural room template selection; cinematic maps such as `ItemStratum_Corridor` are loaded explicitly.
- Do not return early from edge handling solely because `itemDeployment.isActive`; the deployed state must still allow the overworld right edge to enter `ItemStratum_Corridor`.
- Do not leave `pendingInventoryHint` armed after the player equips `sword_rustborn`; otherwise the open-inventory tutorial can reappear after the goal has already been completed.
- If the project moves to true walkable same-scene streaming from Shaft into ItemStratum, update `DEC-039` because Stratum 1 entry currently remains a scene-bound entry flow.
