## Session - Player 2x1 Virtual Slopes

- Added player-only 2x1 virtual slope collision inferred from unchanged IntGrid low/low/high or high/low/low surface patterns.
- Walking, running, ground dash, and air dash now use slope-aware pixel sweep so 2-tile-wide, 1-tile-high transitions move diagonally instead of snapping or blocking.
- Kept 1x1 slopes disabled by rule; ledge snap and dash corner correction remain fallback for non-2x1 geometry.
- Fixed downhill traversal by excluding the inferred slope support/cap cells from slope-mode wall blocking; otherwise the high-side support behind the player stops descent.
